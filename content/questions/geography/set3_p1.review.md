## Agriculture as an Economic Activity

_Syllabus: Subsistence and commercial farming; shifting cultivation, bush fallowing, plantations, pastoralism, irrigation; factors and problems_

- **A.** moves to a new piece of land when the soil becomes exhausted  ✅
- **B.** uses fertilisers to farm the same plot every year
- **C.** rears cattle in search of pasture
- **D.** grows one cash crop on a large estate

Land is cleared by slash and burn, farmed for two or three years, then abandoned for a new area.

**Why the other options are wrong**

### 26. `geo-tt-01`
