### 9. `geo-es-04`

When it is 12.00 noon at Greenwich (0°), what is the local time at a place on longitude 30°E?

- **A.** 2.00 p.m.  ✅
- **B.** 10.00 a.m.
- **C.** 1.00 p.m.
- **D.** 12.30 p.m.

15° of longitude = 1 hour. 30° = 2 hours, and east is ahead, so 12.00 noon + 2 hours = 2.00 p.m.

**Why the other options are wrong**

- 10.00 a.m.  
  Places east of Greenwich are ahead; subtracting gives the time at 30°W.
- 1.00 p.m.  
  Each hour is 15° of longitude, so 30° is two hours, not one.
- 12.30 p.m.  
  That treats 1° as one minute; each degree is four minutes.

### 16. `geo-pt-01`

- **B.** conservative plate boundary

Where plates move apart, magma rises and forms new crust and an ocean ridge.

**Why the other options are wrong**

## Erosion and Deposition by Rivers

_Syllabus: River processes; features of the upper, middle and lower courses_

### 26. `geo-rv-01`

The wearing away of a river's bed and banks by the load it carries is called

- **A.** attrition
- **B.** corrasion (abrasion)  ✅
- **C.** hydraulic action
- **D.** solution (corrosion)

In corrasion, stones carried by the river grind against the bed and banks.

**Why the other options are wrong**

- attrition: Attrition is the load wearing itself down as particles knock together.
- hydraulic action: Hydraulic action is the force of the water itself.
- solution (corrosion): Solution is the dissolving of rocks by the water.

**Why the other options are wrong**

Erosion narrows the meander neck; a flood cuts through it and deposition seals off the old loop.
