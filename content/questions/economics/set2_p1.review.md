A commercialised enterprise charges economic prices and aims to make a profit.

**Why the other options are wrong**

- it is sold to private shareholders: That is privatisation.
- it is closed down: Commercialisation keeps it running.
- it receives larger government subsidies: It must reduce its reliance on subsidies.

- **B.** a few large sellers

- **D.** differentiated products with heavy advertising

Identical products, many firms, free entry and perfect knowledge define perfect competition.

### 51. `eco-ms-03`

In the long run, a firm in perfect competition earns

- **A.** abnormal profit
- **B.** a loss
- **C.** no revenue
- **D.** normal profit only  ✅

Free entry and exit push price to the lowest point of average cost.

- a loss: Loss-making firms leave until losses disappear.

### 52. `eco-ms-04`

- **B.** average total cost

- **D.** marginal revenue

If price covers AVC, producing loses less than closing down, which loses all the fixed cost.

A monopolist's marginal revenue curve
