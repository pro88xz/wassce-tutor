### 10. `fm-pf-03`

### 29. `fm-sets-02`

- **A.** $A' \cap B'$  ✅
- **B.** $A' \cup B'$
- **C.** $A \cap B$
- **D.** $A' \cap B$

De Morgan's law: the complement of a union is the intersection of the complements.

**Why the other options are wrong**

- $A' \cap B$: Both sets must be complemented: $(A \cup B)' = A' \cap B'$.
