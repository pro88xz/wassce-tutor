### 1. `phy-units-01`

Which of the following is a fundamental quantity?

- **A.** Force
- **B.** Weight
- **C.** Pressure
- **D.** Electric current  ✅

The seven SI base quantities are length, mass, time, electric current, temperature, amount of substance and luminous intensity. Electric current (the ampere) is one of them.

**Why the other options are wrong**

### 33. `phy-eqm-01`
