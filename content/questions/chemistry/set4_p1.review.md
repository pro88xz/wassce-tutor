### 41. `chem-sd-01`

When a fat is boiled with sodium hydroxide solution, the products are

- **A.** soap and ethanol
- **B.** margarine and water
- **C.** glycerol and fatty acids only

In soap making, the soap is separated from the glycerol solution by adding
