### 17. `agr-an-03`

A farmer wants 100 kg of feed containing 16% crude protein, using maize (10% crude protein) and groundnut cake (40% crude protein). How much of each is needed?

- **B.** 20 kg of maize and 80 kg of groundnut cake
- **C.** 50 kg of maize and 50 kg of groundnut cake
- **D.** 60 kg of maize and 40 kg of groundnut cake

Pearson square: maize 40 − 16 = 24 parts; groundnut cake 16 − 10 = 6 parts; total 30 parts. Maize = 24/30 × 100 = 80 kg; groundnut cake = 6/30 × 100 = 20 kg. Check: 0.8 × 10 + 0.2 × 40 = 8 + 8 = 16%.

**Why the other options are wrong**

- 20 kg of maize and 80 kg of groundnut cake: The amounts are on the wrong feeds; this mix would contain 34% protein.
- 50 kg of maize and 50 kg of groundnut cake: Equal amounts give 25% protein, far more than 16%.
- 60 kg of maize and 40 kg of groundnut cake: This mix gives 22% protein, not 16%.

### 45. `agr-pp-01`

A poultry house is built with its long axis running east to west so that
