### 2. `agr-cp-02`

- **A.** 5
- **B.** 2000
- **C.** 50 000  ✅

- swollen shoot is caused by too much shade: Swollen shoot is a virus disease, not a result of shade.
- fungicides make the disease worse: Fungicides act on fungi; they have no effect on a virus.

- **C.** stop all pests reaching the plants

### 43. `agr-vf-04`

### 44. `agr-vf-05`

Mango is usually propagated by grafting rather than by seed because

- **A.** mango seeds never germinate
- **B.** grafted trees need no care after planting
- **C.** seedling trees cannot produce fruit at all
- **D.** grafted trees bear earlier and give fruit of the same quality as the parent  ✅

Grafted mango trees bear in 3 to 5 years and are true to type. Citrus is propagated by budding for the same reasons.

### 46. `agr-vf-07`

Which practice best reduces post-harvest losses of tomatoes taken to market?

- **A.** Packing them tightly in sacks
- **B.** Picking in the cool morning, packing in crates and transporting quickly  ✅
- **C.** Leaving them in the sun to ripen evenly
- **D.** Harvesting them in the hottest part of the day

Gentle handling, crates instead of sacks, shade and quick transport keep vegetables fresh; drying and processing reduce losses further.

**Why the other options are wrong**

- Packing them tightly in sacks: Sacks crush the fruit, and bruised tomatoes rot quickly.
- Leaving them in the sun to ripen evenly: Sun heats the fruit and speeds up rotting.
- Harvesting them in the hottest part of the day: Fruit picked in the heat spoils faster.
