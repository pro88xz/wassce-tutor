### 1. `maths-indices-01`

Simplify $\dfrac{27^{\frac{2}{3}} \times 8^{-\frac{1}{3}}}{9^{\frac{1}{2}}}$.

- **D.** $6$

$8^{-\frac{1}{3}} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt[3]{8}} = \dfrac{1}{2}$

$9^{\frac{1}{2}} = \sqrt{9} = 3$

**Why the other options are wrong**

- $6$: You took $8^{-\frac{1}{3}}$ as 2. The negative index means the reciprocal: $8^{-\frac{1}{3}} = \dfrac{1}{2}$.

### 47. `maths-probability-02`
