### 16. `maths-matrices-06`

### 20. `maths-matrices-10`

$A$ is a $2 \times 2$ matrix with determinant 5. Find the determinant of $2A$.

- **B.** $10$

**Why the other options are wrong**

- $7$: You added 2 to the determinant.
- $10$: Multiplying a $2 \times 2$ matrix by 2 doubles every entry, so the determinant is multiplied by $2 \times 2 = 4$.
- $25$: 25 is $5^{2}$. The determinant of $2A$ is $2^{2} \times 5$.

### 21. `maths-transformations-06`

**Why the other options are wrong**

- **A.** $3.27$  ✅
- **B.** $5.66$
- **C.** $8$
- **D.** $10.67$

Variance $= \dfrac{32}{3} = 10.67$; standard deviation $= \sqrt{10.67} = 3.27$

Multiplying every value by 2 doubles every distance from the mean, so the standard deviation doubles: $2 \times 3 = 6$. (The variance is multiplied by 4.)
