### 1. `maths-indices-06`

Simplify $\dfrac{a^{5} \times a^{-2}}{a^{4}}$.

- **A.** $a^{3}$
- **B.** $a^{11}$
- **C.** $a^{-\frac{5}{2}}$
- **D.** $\dfrac{1}{a}$  ✅

Multiplying powers: add the indices. Dividing: subtract them.

$a^{5} \times a^{-2} \div a^{4} = a^{5 + (-2) - 4} = a^{-1} = \dfrac{1}{a}$

**Why the other options are wrong**

- $a^{11}$: You added all the indices, $5 + 2 + 4$. Dividing by $a^{4}$ means subtracting 4.
- $a^{-\frac{5}{2}}$: You multiplied the indices, $5 \times (-2) = -10$, then divided by 4. Multiply powers by adding indices.

### 36. `maths-quadratics-06`

Solve $x^{2} = 4x$.

- **A.** $x = 4$ only
- **B.** $x = 2$ or $x = -2$
- **C.** $x = 0$ or $x = -4$
- **D.** $x = 0$ or $x = 4$  ✅

$x(x - 4) = 0$

$x = 0$ or $x = 4$

- $x = 0$ or $x = -4$: $x(x - 4) = 0$ gives $x = 4$, not $-4$.

### 37. `maths-quadratics-07`

Solve $3x^{2} - 10x - 8 = 0$.

- **B.** $x = \frac{2}{3}$ or $x = -4$
- **C.** $x = -2$ or $x = 4$
- **D.** $x = -\frac{4}{3}$ or $x = 2$

Check: $3x^{2} - 12x + 2x - 8 = 3x^{2} - 10x - 8$. ✓

$3x + 2 = 0 \Rightarrow x = -\dfrac{2}{3}$; $x - 4 = 0 \Rightarrow x = 4$.

**Why the other options are wrong**

- $x = \dfrac{2}{3}$ or $x = -4$: The signs are reversed. From $(3x + 2)(x - 4) = 0$: $3x = -2$ and $x = 4$.
- $x = -2$ or $x = 4$: From $3x + 2 = 0$, divide by 3: $x = -\dfrac{2}{3}$, not $-2$.
- $x = -\dfrac{4}{3}$ or $x = 2$: $(3x + 4)(x - 2) = 3x^{2} - 2x - 8$: the middle term is wrong. Check by expanding.

- **C.** $13$

### 39. `maths-quadratics-09`

A rectangle has a perimeter of 26 cm and an area of 40 cm². Find its length (the longer side).

- **A.** $5$ cm
- **B.** $8$ cm  ✅
- **C.** $10$ cm
- **D.** $13$ cm

$l(13 - l) = 40 \Rightarrow l^{2} - 13l + 40 = 0 \Rightarrow (l - 8)(l - 5) = 0$

The sides are 8 cm and 5 cm, so the length is 8 cm.

**Why the other options are wrong**

- $5$ cm: 5 cm is the width, the shorter side.
- $10$ cm: You divided the area by 4. Use $l + w = 13$ and $lw = 40$.
- $13$ cm: 13 cm is the sum of the length and width (half the perimeter).

### 46. `maths-probability-06`

### 48. `maths-probability-08`

- **B.** $\frac{1}{8}$
- **C.** $\frac{1}{6}$
- **D.** $\frac{2}{3}$

- $\dfrac{2}{3}$: You added the probabilities. For independent events happening together, multiply.
