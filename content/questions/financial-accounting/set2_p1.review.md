## The Trial Balance

_Syllabus: Purpose and format of the trial balance; debit and credit balances; correcting a trial balance_

### 1. `fa-tb-01`

The main purpose of a trial balance is to

- **A.** check the arithmetical accuracy of the double entry  ✅
- **B.** find the net profit of the business
- **C.** show the financial position on a date
- **D.** prove that the books contain no errors

Equal debit and credit totals show that the double entry is arithmetically correct.

**Why the other options are wrong**

- find the net profit of the business: Net profit is found in the income statement.
- show the financial position on a date: That is the statement of financial position.
- prove that the books contain no errors: Some errors are not revealed by the trial balance.

### 2. `fa-tb-02`

Which balance appears on the credit side of a trial balance?

- **A.** returns inwards
- **B.** returns outwards  ✅
- **C.** discount allowed
- **D.** drawings

Returns outwards (purchases returns) reduce purchases and are a credit balance.

**Why the other options are wrong**

- returns inwards: Returns inwards reduce sales: a debit balance.
- discount allowed: Discount allowed is an expense: a debit balance.
- drawings: Drawings are a debit balance.

## Errors in Accounting

### 6. `fa-er-01`

A purchase of a computer was debited to the purchases account. This is an error of

- **A.** commission
- **B.** principle  ✅
- **C.** omission
- **D.** original entry

A non-current asset has been treated as an expense: the wrong class of account.

**Why the other options are wrong**

- commission: Commission is posting to the wrong account of the same class.
- omission: Omission is leaving a transaction out completely.
- original entry: Original entry is a wrong amount in the source book.

### 7. `fa-er-02`

Cash received from Bah was credited to Bai's account. This is an error of

- **A.** principle
- **B.** complete reversal
- **C.** commission  ✅
- **D.** compensating

The correct amount was posted to the wrong personal account of the same class.

**Why the other options are wrong**

- principle: Principle is posting to the wrong class of account.
- complete reversal: Complete reversal swaps debit and credit.
- compensating: Compensating errors cancel each other out.

### 8. `fa-er-03`

Which error will cause the trial balance totals to disagree?

- **A.** a sale omitted from the books entirely
- **B.** a sale of 2,450 recorded as 2,540 in the day book
- **C.** a payment posted to the wrong supplier's account
- **D.** wages posted to the cash book but not to the wages account  ✅

Only one side of the entry was made, so the debits are short.

**Why the other options are wrong**

- a sale omitted from the books entirely: Omission leaves both sides out.

## The Suspense Account

### 11. `fa-su-01`

- **A.** a credit balance of NLe 700

- **D.** no balance

Difference = 90,300 − 89,600 = 700, added to the short (debit) side.

**Why the other options are wrong**
