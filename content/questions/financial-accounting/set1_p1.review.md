## The Accounting Equation

_Syllabus: Assets, liabilities and capital; the accounting equation; effect of transactions_

### 11. `fa-eq-01`

The accounting equation is

- **A.** Assets = Capital − Liabilities
- **B.** Capital = Assets + Liabilities
- **C.** Assets = Capital + Liabilities  ✅
- **D.** Liabilities = Assets + Capital

Everything the business owns is financed by the owner (capital) or by outsiders (liabilities).

**Why the other options are wrong**

- Assets = Capital − Liabilities: Liabilities are added to capital, not subtracted.
- Capital = Assets + Liabilities: Capital equals assets minus liabilities.
- Liabilities = Assets + Capital: Liabilities equal assets minus capital.

### 31. `fa-pc-01`

Reimbursing exactly what was spent restores the cash in hand to the imprest.
