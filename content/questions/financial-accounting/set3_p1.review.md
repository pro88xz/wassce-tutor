## Control Accounts

_Syllabus: Sales and purchases ledger control accounts; sources of entries; set-offs; reconciliation_

### 1. `fa-ct-01`

The credit sales figure in a sales ledger control account is taken from the

- **A.** sales day book  ✅
- **B.** cash book
- **C.** purchases day book
- **D.** returns inwards book

Control accounts are written up from totals in the books of original entry.

**Why the other options are wrong**

- cash book: The cash book gives receipts, not credit sales.
- purchases day book: That gives credit purchases.
- returns inwards book: That gives returns inwards.

### 2. `fa-ct-02`

Which item appears on the credit side of a sales ledger control account?

- **A.** credit sales
- **B.** discount allowed  ✅
- **C.** dishonoured cheques
- **D.** discount received

Discount allowed reduces what customers owe, so it is credited.

**Why the other options are wrong**

- credit sales: Credit sales are a debit.
- dishonoured cheques: Dishonoured cheques are a debit: the customer owes again.
- discount received: Discount received belongs in the purchases ledger control account.

## Departmental Accounts

### 6. `fa-dp-01`

The most suitable basis for apportioning rent between departments is

- **A.** sales
- **B.** floor area  ✅
- **C.** number of employees
- **D.** value of equipment

Rent is paid for space, so floor area is the fairest basis.

**Why the other options are wrong**

- **A.** an independent branch

Head office supplies and controls a dependent branch and keeps its accounts.

**Why the other options are wrong**

### 12. `fa-bn-02`

Everything head office sends or pays for the branch is debited to the branch account.

**Why the other options are wrong**

### 13. `fa-bn-03`

Opening branch receivables NLe 3,000; cash from receivables NLe 25,000; closing receivables NLe 5,000. Branch credit sales are

### 14. `fa-bn-04`

Goods are sent to a branch at cost plus 25%. The loading is what fraction of the selling price?

- **B.** one-fifth  ✅

- **D.** one-half

Cost 100, selling price 125; loading 25 ÷ 125 = 1/5.

**Why the other options are wrong**

### 26. `fa-bx-01`

The person who draws a bill of exchange is usually the

### 27. `fa-bx-02`

- **A.** NLe 8,800
- **B.** NLe 9,700

- **D.** NLe 10,200

Discount = 10,000 × 12% × 2/12 = 200; proceeds = 9,800.

**Why the other options are wrong**

### 28. `fa-bx-03`

### 30. `fa-bx-05`
