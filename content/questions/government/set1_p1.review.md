## Meaning and Nature of Government

_Syllabus: Government as institution, process and subject; functions of government; power, authority, legitimacy, influence, political culture and socialisation_

### 1. `gov-mn-01`

Government as an institution refers to

- **A.** the organs that make, carry out and interpret the laws of a state  ✅
- **B.** the academic study of the state, its organs and how power is used
- **C.** the activity or process of ruling a society and making decisions for it
- **D.** the citizens who vote in elections to choose their representatives

As an institution, government means the legislature, executive and judiciary that make, enforce and interpret laws.

**Why the other options are wrong**

- the academic study of the state, its organs and how power is used: That is government as an academic subject.
- the activity or process of ruling a society and making decisions for it: That is government as a process or art.
- the citizens who vote in elections to choose their representatives: Voters are the electorate, not the institutions of government.

Authority is legitimate power: the right to command that people accept.

**Why the other options are wrong**

- force: Force is the use of physical coercion, not accepted right.
- influence: Influence is the ability to persuade without the right to command.
- sovereignty: Sovereignty is the supreme power of the state, not acceptance by the governed.

### 7. `gov-ss-02`

Sovereignty means

- **A.** the right of all adult citizens to vote freely in periodic elections
- **B.** the power of the courts to interpret the laws and settle disputes
- **C.** supreme power of a state over its people and territory, free from external control  ✅
- **D.** a group of people who share a common culture, language and history

Sovereignty is the supreme and final legal authority of the state, internally and externally.

**Why the other options are wrong**

- the right of all adult citizens to vote freely in periodic elections: That is the franchise.
- the power of the courts to interpret the laws and settle disputes: That is judicial power, only part of state authority.
- a group of people who share a common culture, language and history: That describes a nation.

In a unitary state the central government is supreme; local bodies get only the powers it delegates.
