**Accounting for bills of exchange** uses two accounts: **bills receivable**, an **asset** in the books of the **drawer** (the person who will receive payment), and **bills payable**, a **liability** in the books of the **acceptor** (the person who must pay). When a bill is accepted, the personal debt is **transferred** from the customer's or supplier's account to the bills account. Further entries record payment, **discounting**, **endorsement** and **dishonour**. This lesson sets out every entry, with worked examples in the books of both parties.

## Learning objectives

By the end of this lesson you should be able to:

- record bills receivable in the drawer's books
- record bills payable in the acceptor's books
- record a bill held to maturity, discounted or endorsed
- record the dishonour of a bill and noting charges
- explain where bills appear in the statement of financial position

## In the drawer's books (bills receivable)

| Event | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---|
| Customer accepts a bill | **Bills receivable** | Customer |
| Bill held and paid on the due date | Bank | Bills receivable |
| Bill discounted with a bank | Bank (proceeds) and **discounting charges** | Bills receivable |
| Bill endorsed to a creditor | Creditor | Bills receivable |
| Bill held by the drawer is **dishonoured** | Customer (plus noting charges) | Bills receivable (and bank for noting charges) |
| **Discounted** bill dishonoured | Customer (plus noting charges) | **Bank** (the bank recovers its money) |

## In the acceptor's books (bills payable)

| Event | Debit | Credit |
|---|---|---|
| Accepts a bill drawn by a supplier | Supplier | **Bills payable** |
| Pays the bill on the due date | Bills payable | Bank |
| Dishonours the bill | Bills payable | Supplier (plus noting charges, debited to noting charges) |

## Worked example

:::callout{type=worked-example}
**On 1 March 2026, Sesay sold goods for NLe 10,000 on credit to Kamara. On the same day Kamara accepted a three-month bill drawn by Sesay for that amount. On 1 April, Sesay discounted the bill with his bank at 12% a year. On the due date the bill was honoured.**

**Sesay's books (drawer)**

| Date | Particulars | Dr NLe | Cr NLe |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mar 1 | Kamara | 10,000 | |
| | Sales | | 10,000 |
| | *Goods sold on credit* | | |
| Mar 1 | Bills receivable | 10,000 | |
| | Kamara | | 10,000 |
| | *Kamara's acceptance received* | | |
| Apr 1 | Bank | 9,800 | |
| | Discounting charges | 200 | |
| | Bills receivable | | 10,000 |
| | *Bill discounted at 12% for 2 months* | | |

When the bill is honoured, Kamara pays the **bank**; Sesay makes **no further entry**.

**Kamara's books (acceptor)**

| Date | Particulars | Dr NLe | Cr NLe |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mar 1 | Purchases | 10,000 | |
| | Sesay | | 10,000 |
| Mar 1 | Sesay | 10,000 | |
| | Bills payable | | 10,000 |
| Jun 4 | Bills payable | 10,000 | |
| | Bank | | 10,000 |
:::

The due date is 1 June plus three days of grace, 4 June.

## Dishonour

:::callout{type=worked-example}
**Suppose instead the discounted bill above was dishonoured on the due date and the bank paid noting charges of NLe 100. Record the entries in Sesay's books.**

| Particulars | Dr NLe | Cr NLe |
|---|---|---|
| Kamara | 10,100 | |
| Bank | | 10,100 |
| *Discounted bill dishonoured; noting charges 100* | | |

The bank takes back the face value and the noting charges from Sesay's account, and Kamara again **owes Sesay** NLe 10,100.
:::

## In the final accounts

- **Bills receivable** on hand at the year end: a **current asset**.
- **Bills payable** outstanding: a **current liability**.
- **Discounting charges** and **noting charges** paid: **expenses** (noting charges are usually recovered from the defaulting acceptor).
- A bill **discounted** but not yet due may be shown as a **contingent liability** in a note: if the acceptor fails to pay, the drawer must refund the bank.

:::callout{type=common-mistake}
When a **discounted** bill is dishonoured, the drawer **credits the bank**, not bills receivable: the bills receivable account was already cleared when the bill was discounted, and it is the bank that reclaims its money.
:::

## Key points

- **Bills receivable** (drawer): an asset; **bills payable** (acceptor): a liability.
- On acceptance the debt moves from the **personal account** to the **bills account**.
- **Discounting:** debit bank and discounting charges, credit bills receivable.
- **Dishonour:** the customer owes again, plus **noting charges**; for a discounted bill, **credit the bank**.

## WASSCE examination focus

:::callout{type=waec-tests}
Expect to **record the drawing, acceptance, discounting, endorsement, payment and dishonour of bills** in journal or ledger form, in the books of the drawer and the acceptor. Work out discounting charges on the time remaining and check due dates.
:::

## Quick check

1. What entry does the drawer make when a customer accepts a bill?
2. What entry does the acceptor make when paying the bill?
3. A bill of NLe 5,000 is endorsed to a creditor, Bangura. Give the entry in the drawer's books.
4. Where do bills payable appear in the statement of financial position?
5. A bill held by the drawer is dishonoured. Give the entry.

**Answers:**

1. Debit bills receivable; credit the customer.
2. Debit bills payable; credit bank.
3. Debit Bangura 5,000; credit bills receivable 5,000.
4. As a current liability.
5. Debit the customer (with any noting charges); credit bills receivable (and bank for noting charges paid).
