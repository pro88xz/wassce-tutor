Not long ago, a trader in Kailahun who needed to reach a supplier in Freetown had to send a letter or travel there. Today the same trader calls, texts or sends a WhatsApp voice note in seconds, and pays by mobile money. This lesson looks at the services that carry business messages: the post office, courier companies and telecommunications.

## Learning objectives

By the end of this lesson you should be able to:

- describe the services of the post office
- explain courier services and compare them with postal services
- describe telecommunication services and their importance to business
- explain the use of the internet, email and social media in business
- state the problems of communication services in Sierra Leone

## Postal services

In Sierra Leone, postal services are run by **SALPOST (Sierra Leone Postal Services Limited)**.

| Service | What it offers |
|---|---|
| **Ordinary mail** | Letters and postcards delivered at standard rates |
| **Registered post** | Mail recorded at posting and signed for on delivery; **compensation** if lost; used for valuable or important items |
| **Recorded delivery** | Proof of posting and delivery, but little or no compensation |
| **Parcel post** | Carries packages within the country and abroad |
| **Express mail (EMS)** | Faster, priority delivery with tracking |
| **Post office boxes** | A private box at the post office where mail is collected; a secure business address |
| **Poste restante** | Mail kept at a post office for travellers to collect |
| **Cash on delivery (COD)** | The post office collects payment from the buyer on delivery and passes it to the seller |
| **Postal orders and money orders** | Safe ways to send small sums of money by post |
| **Business reply service** | Firms pay the postage on customers' replies |
| **Franking machines** | Firms print postage on mail themselves instead of buying stamps |
| **Sale of stamps and forms** | Postage stamps; some government forms |

**Importance to business:** a cheap way to send documents, catalogues and goods; registered post gives proof and security; COD lets mail-order traders collect payment; PO boxes give a secure address.

## Courier services

:::callout{type=definition}
A **courier service** is a private company that collects documents and parcels and delivers them quickly, **door to door**, usually with **tracking** and proof of delivery.
:::

| Postal service | Courier service |
|---|---|
| Usually run or licensed by the state | Usually private companies |
| Cheaper | More expensive |
| Slower | Faster, often next day |
| Delivery to box or address; customer often collects | **Door-to-door** collection and delivery |
| Limited tracking | Full **tracking** and signature on delivery |

## Telecommunication services

**Telecommunication** is communication over a distance by electronic means.

| Service | Use in business |
|---|---|
| **Mobile telephone** | Instant calls to customers and suppliers; SMS for orders, alerts and receipts |
| **Mobile money** | Paying suppliers, receiving payment and transferring money by phone |
| **Fixed-line telephone** | Office lines; less common now |
| **Internet** | Websites, online shops, research, online banking |
| **Email** | Fast written messages with attachments (invoices, catalogues); keeps a record |
| **Social media and WhatsApp** | Advertising, customer service, groups for traders, photos of goods |
| **Video calls and conferencing** | Meetings without travel |
| **Radio and television** | Advertising and public announcements; radio reaches rural areas |
| **Fax and telex** (older) | Sent copies of documents over telephone lines; now replaced by email |

:::callout{type=local-example}
Sierra Leone's main mobile networks include **Orange, Africell and Qcell**, and the state-owned **Sierratel** provides fixed lines and internet. The **National Telecommunications Commission (NATCOM)** licenses and regulates the sector. Mobile money services such as **Orange Money** and **Afrimoney** are now used by traders across the country, from Freetown wholesalers to rural market women.
:::

## Advantages of modern telecommunication to business

1. **Speed**: messages and payments arrive instantly.
2. **Reach**: customers anywhere, including abroad.
3. **Low cost** compared with travel.
4. **Two-way**: instant feedback and negotiation.
5. **Records**: emails and SMS can be kept as evidence.
6. **New markets**: online selling, social media advertising.
7. **Saves time**: fewer journeys to banks, suppliers and offices.

## Problems of communication services in Sierra Leone

1. **Poor network coverage** in rural areas.
2. **Unreliable electricity** for phones, computers and equipment.
3. **High cost** of data and calls for small traders.
4. **Slow postal delivery** and lack of street addresses in many areas.
5. **Fraud and scams**: fake mobile money messages, hacking.
6. **Illiteracy**, which limits use of written and electronic messages.
7. **Theft and loss** of mail and parcels.

**Solutions:** expand network coverage; improve power supply; reduce taxes on data and phones; introduce a national address system; educate users on fraud; regulate operators to improve service.

:::callout{type=common-mistake}
**Registered post** gives **compensation** if the item is lost; **recorded delivery** gives only **proof** of posting and delivery. Do not mix them up.
:::

## Key terms

:::callout{type=definition title="Key terms"}
- **Registered post**: mail recorded at posting and signed for on delivery, with compensation if lost.
- **Recorded delivery**: mail with proof of posting and delivery, but little or no compensation.
- **Poste restante**: mail kept at a post office for travellers to collect.
- **Business reply service**: a postal service in which a firm pays the postage on its customers' replies.
- **Franking machine**: a machine that prints postage on a firm's mail instead of stamps.
:::

## Key points

- The **post office (SALPOST)** offers ordinary, registered and express mail, parcels, PO boxes, COD and money orders.
- **Courier services** are faster, door-to-door and tracked, but dearer than post.
- **Telecommunication**: mobile phones, mobile money, internet, email, social media, radio and TV.
- Modern communication brings **speed, reach, low cost and records**, but faces **poor coverage, power cuts, cost and fraud**.

## WASSCE examination focus

:::callout{type=waec-tests}
Expect to **list and explain services of the post office**, to **distinguish postal and courier services**, and to **explain how telecommunication and the internet help business**. A question on problems of communication in your country is common. Name a real service for each point.
:::
