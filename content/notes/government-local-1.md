Who cleans the market in Bo, maintains feeder roads in Koinadugu or collects property rates in Freetown? Not the President or Parliament, but **local councils**. Local government brings decisions and services closer to the people who use them. This lesson explains what local government is, its types and features, and the structure and functions of local government in Sierra Leone.

## Learning objectives

By the end of this lesson you should be able to:

- define local government and state its features
- distinguish local government from field administration
- explain the reasons for local government
- describe the structure of local government in Sierra Leone
- state the functions of local councils

## What is local government?

:::callout{type=definition}
**Local government** is government at the **local level**, by **elected local councils** with legal powers and resources to provide services and make decisions for a defined area such as a district, city or town.
:::

**Features of local government:**

1. A **defined area of authority** (district, city or municipality).
2. **Legal existence** under an Act of Parliament.
3. **Elected councillors** and leaders.
4. **Power to raise revenue** through local taxes, rates and fees.
5. **Power to make bye-laws** for its area.
6. **Its own staff** and administration.
7. **Supervision** by the central government.

### Local government and field administration

| Local government | Field administration (deconcentration) |
|---|---|
| Elected councils with legal powers | Officials of central ministries posted to districts |
| Accountable to local voters | Accountable to the ministry at the centre |
| Raises some of its own revenue | Funded by the central budget |
| Example: Freetown City Council | Example: a District Education Office |

## Reasons for local government

- **Brings government closer** to the people.
- **Encourages participation** and political education.
- **Trains local leaders** who may later serve nationally.
- **Reduces the workload** of the central government.
- **Local knowledge**: councils understand local needs best.
- **Faster delivery** of services and development.
- **Mobilises local resources** for development.

## Local government in Sierra Leone

![Two strands under the Ministry of Local Government: elected local councils (district councils and city or municipal councils, led by directly elected mayors or chairpersons, with wards and ward committees) and traditional authority (Paramount Chiefs and chiefdom councils in 190 chiefdoms, section chiefs, town and village chiefs)](https://wasscetutor.com/img/government/sl-local-government.svg)

*Figure 1. Local administration in Sierra Leone. WASSCE Tutor diagram.*

- **Local councils**, revived by the **Local Government Act 2004** after decades of suspension, and now governed by the **Local Government Act 2022**.
  - **District councils** in rural areas, led by a **chairperson**.
  - **City and municipal councils** in towns, led by a **mayor**, for example Freetown City Council.
- **Mayors and chairpersons** are **directly elected** by voters; **councillors** represent wards (elected by district block PR in 2023).
- Each **ward** has a **ward committee** that links the council with communities.
- Councils serve a **five-year** term.
- Alongside the councils, **190 chiefdoms** in the provinces are headed by **Paramount Chiefs** with **chiefdom councils**, and below them section, town and village chiefs.
- The **Ministry of Local Government** oversees the system, and grants from central government support council budgets.

## Functions of local councils

1. **Sanitation**: refuse collection, markets and public toilets.
2. **Primary education and primary health care** facilities (devolved functions).
3. **Feeder roads**, streets and drainage.
4. **Water supply** in some areas.
5. **Markets**, lorry parks and slaughterhouses.
6. **Collecting local revenue**: rates, licences, market dues.
7. **Making bye-laws** for their areas.
8. **Development planning** for the locality.
9. **Registration** of births and deaths and issuing of trade licences.
10. **Maintaining** recreation grounds, cemeteries and public places.

:::callout{type=local-example}
After the civil war, Sierra Leone made **decentralisation** part of rebuilding the state. Local councils, abolished in **1972**, were restored by the **Local Government Act 2004**, with the first council elections that year. Functions such as primary health, primary education and agriculture extension were **devolved** to councils, together with grants to fund them. Today there are **22 local councils** across the country.
:::

:::callout{type=common-mistake}
Local councils and chiefdoms are not the same thing. **Councils** are **elected bodies** created by statute; **chiefdoms** are **traditional authorities** headed by Paramount Chiefs. In Sierra Leone the two work side by side in the provinces, which sometimes leads to overlapping roles.
:::

## Key terms

:::callout{type=definition title="Key terms"}
- **Bye-law**: a law made by a local council for its own area.
- **Councillor**: an elected member of a local council who represents a ward.
- **Local Government Act 2004**: the Act that revived Sierra Leone's local councils after decades of suspension; replaced by the Local Government Act 2022.
:::

## Key points

- **Local government** is government at the local level by **elected councils** with legal powers and revenue.
- It differs from **field administration**, where central officials work in the districts.
- Reasons: **closeness to people, participation, training leaders, local knowledge, faster services**.
- Sierra Leone: **Local Government Act 2004** (revived councils), now the **2022 Act**; **district** and **city/municipal councils**; **22 councils**; **directly elected mayors and chairpersons**; **190 chiefdoms**.
- Functions: **sanitation, primary health and education, feeder roads, markets, revenue, bye-laws, planning**.

## Quick check

1. Define local government.
2. Which Act revived local councils in Sierra Leone after the civil war?
3. Who heads a city council? Who heads a district council?
4. State three functions of local councils.

**Answers:**

1. Government at the local level by elected councils that have legal powers and resources to provide services and make decisions for a defined area.
2. The Local Government Act 2004 (now replaced by the Local Government Act 2022).
3. A mayor heads a city council; a chairperson heads a district council.
4. Any three: sanitation; primary education and health facilities; feeder roads and drainage; water supply; markets and lorry parks; revenue collection; bye-laws; development planning; registration of births and deaths; licences; maintaining public places.
