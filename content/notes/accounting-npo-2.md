The **income and expenditure account** of a club is prepared from the receipts and payments account plus information about **subscriptions in arrears and in advance**, **bar inventory**, **equipment and depreciation**, and **accrued or prepaid expenses**. The key working is the **subscriptions account**, which finds the subscription **income for the year**. Together with the **accumulated fund** at the start, these produce a full set of final accounts for the club. This lesson works through a complete example.

## Learning objectives

By the end of this lesson you should be able to:

- prepare a subscriptions account and find subscription income for the year
- prepare a bar trading account
- calculate the opening accumulated fund
- prepare an income and expenditure account
- prepare the statement of financial position of a club

## Subscription income

$$\text{Subscription income} = \text{Received} - \text{Opening arrears} + \text{Opening in advance} + \text{Closing arrears} - \text{Closing in advance}$$

:::callout{type=worked-example}
**Kono Youth Sports Club (continued from the previous lesson). Subscriptions received in 2026 were NLe 18,000. On 1 January, NLe 1,000 was in arrears and NLe 500 had been paid in advance for 2026. On 31 December, NLe 1,500 was in arrears and NLe 800 had been paid in advance for 2027. Other information: bar inventory 1 January 2,000, 31 December 3,000; equipment at 1 January valued at 20,000; depreciation 10% a year on all equipment at the year end.**
:::

**Subscription income:**

$18\,000 - 1\,000 + 500 + 1\,500 - 800 = 18\,200$

- Subtract **1,000**: received this year but belonging to **last** year.
- Add **500**: paid last year for **this** year.
- Add **1,500**: belongs to **this** year but not yet received.
- Subtract **800**: received this year for **next** year.

**Bar trading account:**

- Bar sales $30\,000$
- Cost of bar sales $= 2\,000 + 21\,000 - 3\,000 = 20\,000$
- **Bar profit** $= 30\,000 - 20\,000 = 10\,000$

**Depreciation:** equipment $20\,000 + 8\,000 = 28\,000$; depreciation $= 10\% \times 28\,000 = 2\,800$.

**Accumulated fund at 1 January 2026:**

| Assets and liabilities at 1 January | NLe |
|---|---|
| Cash | 4,000 |
| Equipment | 20,000 |
| Bar inventory | 2,000 |
| Subscriptions in arrears | 1,000 |
| Less subscriptions in advance | (500) |
| **Accumulated fund** | **26,500** |

## Income and expenditure account

**Kono Youth Sports Club: income and expenditure account for the year ended 31 December 2026**

| Income | NLe |
|---|---|
| Subscriptions | 18,200 |
| Donations | 5,000 |
| Bar profit | 10,000 |
| **Total income** | **33,200** |

| Expenditure | NLe |
|---|---|
| Wages | 9,000 |
| Rent | 6,000 |
| Depreciation of equipment | 2,800 |
| **Total expenditure** | **17,800** |
| **Surplus** | **15,400** |

## Statement of financial position

| At 31 December 2026 | NLe |
|---|---|
| Equipment at cost/valuation | 28,000 |
| Less depreciation | (2,800) |
| **Equipment** | **25,200** |
| Bar inventory | 3,000 |
| Subscriptions in arrears | 1,500 |
| Cash | 13,000 |
| **Total assets** | **42,700** |
| Less subscriptions in advance (current liability) | (800) |
| **Net assets** | **41,900** |
| Accumulated fund 1 January | 26,500 |
| Add surplus | 15,400 |
| **Accumulated fund 31 December** | **41,900** |

**Check:** $26\,500 + 15\,400 = 41\,900$ = net assets ✓

## Treatment summary

| Item | Income and expenditure account | Statement of financial position |
|---|---|---|
| subscriptions in arrears (end) | added to income | current asset |
| subscriptions in advance (end) | deducted from income | current liability |
| bar | bar profit only | bar inventory as current asset |
| equipment bought | depreciation only | non-current asset |
| donations (small, general) | income | — |

:::callout{type=common-mistake}
Do not use the **cash received** for subscriptions as the subscription income. Adjust it for arrears and amounts in advance at **both** the start and the end of the year; the subscriptions account does this systematically.
:::

## Key terms

:::callout{type=definition title="Key terms"}
- **Accumulated fund**: the capital of a non-profit organisation: its assets minus its liabilities.
- **Surplus**: the excess of income over expenditure, added to the accumulated fund.
- **Deficit**: the excess of expenditure over income, deducted from the accumulated fund.
- **Subscriptions in arrears**: members' fees still owing at the end of the period, a current asset.
:::

## Key points

- **Subscription income = received − opening arrears + opening in advance + closing arrears − closing in advance.**
- A **bar trading account** finds the **bar profit**.
- **Opening accumulated fund** = assets − liabilities at the start.
- **Surplus** is added to the accumulated fund; the statement of financial position must agree.

## WASSCE examination focus

:::callout{type=waec-tests}
A standard Paper 2 question gives a **receipts and payments account plus notes** and asks for the **subscriptions account, bar trading account, accumulated fund, income and expenditure account and statement of financial position**. Present each working separately and check the final balance.
:::

## Quick check

1. Subscriptions received NLe 12,000; opening arrears NLe 600; closing arrears NLe 900. Find subscription income (no amounts in advance).
2. Where do subscriptions paid in advance at the year end appear?
3. Bar sales NLe 15,000; opening bar inventory NLe 1,000; bar purchases NLe 9,000; closing NLe 1,500. Find bar profit.
4. How is the accumulated fund at the start found?
5. Total income NLe 25,000; total expenditure NLe 27,500. What is the result?

**Answers:**

1. $12\,000 - 600 + 900 = 12\,300$: NLe 12,300.
2. As a current liability in the statement of financial position (and deducted from subscription income).
3. Cost $= 1\,000 + 9\,000 - 1\,500 = 8\,500$; bar profit $= 6\,500$: NLe 6,500.
4. By a statement of affairs: assets minus liabilities at the start of the year.
5. A deficit of NLe 2,500.
