# Legal aspects of business: contracts, agency and hire purchase

## Learning objectives
Explain why businesses operate within a legal framework, identify basic elements of a contract and describe agency and hire purchase.

## Law and commercial activity
Business law provides rules for agreements, ownership, employment, consumer transactions, company organisation and dispute settlement. It helps define the rights and duties of traders, customers, employees, suppliers and other parties. Ignoring legal requirements can lead to disputes, penalties, financial loss and reputational damage.

The precise rules differ by jurisdiction and can change. Students should learn the principles required by the syllabus while recognising that a real transaction in Sierra Leone must comply with current Sierra Leone law and applicable regulations.

## Basic elements of a contract
A contract is an agreement recognised by law as creating enforceable obligations. Common elements include:
- **Offer:** a clear proposal on terms capable of acceptance.
- **Acceptance:** an unqualified agreement to the offer's terms, communicated as required.
- **Consideration:** something of value exchanged, where required by the applicable legal system.
- **Intention to create legal relations:** the parties intend the agreement to have legal consequences.
- **Capacity:** parties have legal ability to enter the agreement.
- **Legality and certainty:** the purpose and terms must be lawful and sufficiently clear.

A quotation or advertisement is not always an offer; its legal effect depends on wording and circumstances. A contract may be written, oral or formed by conduct, although some transactions must meet specific formal requirements.

## Agency
Agency is a relationship in which an agent is authorised to act on behalf of a principal in dealings with third parties. An agent may negotiate or conclude transactions within the authority granted. The principal may be bound by acts performed within the agent's actual or apparent authority, subject to applicable law. Agents generally owe duties such as acting within authority, following lawful instructions, exercising reasonable care and accounting for money received.

## Hire purchase
Hire purchase is an arrangement in which a customer obtains possession and use of goods after paying an initial deposit and agrees to pay the balance in instalments. Ownership normally passes only after the required payments and conditions are completed. The agreement should state the cash price, deposit, instalments, total hire-purchase price, duration, fees and consequences of default. Consumer-protection and credit rules may apply.

## WASSCE examination focus
For hire purchase, distinguish possession from ownership. The hirer may use goods before becoming the owner. For contract questions, identify the elements and apply each to the facts rather than merely listing legal vocabulary.
