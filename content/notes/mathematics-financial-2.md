Put NLe 10,000 in a savings account at 10% a year. With simple interest you earn NLe 1,000 every year. With **compound interest** you earn interest on your interest, and after ten years you have nearly NLe 6,000 more. This lesson covers compound interest, depreciation, and the everyday business calculations WASSCE tests: profit and loss, discount, commission and hire purchase.

## Learning objectives

By the end of this lesson you should be able to:

- calculate compound interest, including interest compounded more than once a year
- compare simple and compound interest
- calculate depreciation
- calculate profit and loss, including percentage profit and loss
- solve problems on discount, commission and hire purchase

## Compound interest

The amount after $n$ years when $P$ is invested at $r\%$ per year, compounded yearly, is

$$A = P\left(1 + \frac{r}{100}\right)^n \qquad \text{compound interest} = A - P$$

:::callout{type=worked-example}
**Find the compound interest on NLe 10,000 at 10% per year for 3 years.**

$A = 10\,000 \times 1.1^3 = 10\,000 \times 1.331 = $ NLe 13,310.

Compound interest $= 13\,310 - 10\,000 = $ NLe 3,310 (simple interest would give only NLe 3,000).
:::

### Compounding more than once a year

If interest is added $k$ times a year, use rate $\dfrac{r}{k}\%$ per period and $nk$ periods.

:::callout{type=worked-example}
**Find the amount when NLe 5,000 is invested for 1 year at 8% per year compounded half-yearly.**

Rate per half-year $= 4\%$, number of periods $= 2$: $A = 5\,000 \times 1.04^2 = 5\,000 \times 1.0816 = $ NLe 5,408.
:::

## Depreciation

Depreciation is a **fall in value** over time. With a constant percentage rate,

$$\text{value after } n \text{ years} = P\left(1 - \frac{r}{100}\right)^n$$

:::callout{type=worked-example}
**A car bought for NLe 200,000 depreciates by 15% per year. Find its value after 2 years.**

$200\,000 \times 0.85^2 = 200\,000 \times 0.7225 = $ NLe 144,500.
:::

## Profit and loss

$$\text{profit} = \text{selling price} - \text{cost price} \qquad \text{percentage profit} = \frac{\text{profit}}{\text{cost price}} \times 100\%$$

Loss and percentage loss are found the same way when the selling price is below the cost price.

:::callout{type=worked-example}
**(a) A trader buys a radio for NLe 800 and sells it for NLe 1,000. Find the percentage profit. (b) What selling price gives a 20% profit on a cost of NLe 1,500? (c) An item costing NLe 2,500 is sold for NLe 2,000. Find the percentage loss.**

**(a)** Profit $= 200$; $\dfrac{200}{800} \times 100 = 25\%$.

**(b)** $1\,500 \times 1.20 = $ NLe 1,800.

**(c)** Loss $= 500$; $\dfrac{500}{2\,500} \times 100 = 20\%$.
:::

:::callout{type=common-mistake}
Percentage profit and loss are calculated on the **cost price**, not the selling price, unless the question says otherwise.
:::

## Discount and commission

- A **discount** is a reduction from the marked price: a 15% discount on NLe 1,200 leaves $1\,200 \times 0.85 = $ NLe 1,020.
- **Commission** is a percentage of sales paid to an agent: 5% commission on sales of NLe 40,000 is NLe 2,000.

## Hire purchase

Hire purchase lets a buyer pay a **deposit** now and the rest in **instalments**, usually costing more than the cash price.

:::callout{type=local-example}
**A fridge in a Freetown shop costs NLe 12,000 cash. On hire purchase the buyer pays a deposit of NLe 3,000 and 12 monthly instalments of NLe 850. How much more does hire purchase cost?**

Hire purchase price $= 3\,000 + 12 \times 850 = 3\,000 + 10\,200 = $ NLe 13,200.

Extra cost $= 13\,200 - 12\,000 = $ NLe 1,200, which is 10% of the cash price.
:::

## Key points

- **Compound interest:** $A = P\left(1 + \tfrac{r}{100}\right)^n$; interest $= A - P$.
- Compounded $k$ times a year: rate $\tfrac{r}{k}\%$, $nk$ periods.
- **Depreciation:** $P\left(1 - \tfrac{r}{100}\right)^n$.
- **Percentage profit or loss** is on the **cost price**.
- **Discount** reduces the marked price; **commission** is a percentage of sales; **hire purchase** = deposit + instalments.

## WASSCE examination focus

:::callout{type=waec-tests}
Expect **compound interest and depreciation over 2 or 3 years**, **comparisons of simple and compound interest**, **percentage profit and loss**, and **hire purchase and commission** problems. Give money answers to the nearest leone (or as the question requires) and state the units.
:::

## Practice

Try each question first, then open the answer to check your working.

**1.** Find the amount after 2 years when NLe 8,000 is invested at 5% compound interest.

:::reveal{title="Show the answer"}
$8\,000 \times 1.05^2 = 8\,000 \times 1.1025 = $ NLe 8,820.
:::

**2.** A machine worth NLe 50,000 depreciates by 10% a year. Find its value after 2 years.

:::reveal{title="Show the answer"}
$50\,000 \times 0.9^2 = $ NLe 40,500.
:::

**3.** A bag bought for NLe 600 is sold at a 15% loss. Find the selling price.

:::reveal{title="Show the answer"}
$600 \times 0.85 = $ NLe 510.
:::

**4.** An agent earns 4% commission. Find the commission on sales of NLe 75,000.

:::reveal{title="Show the answer"}
NLe 3,000.
:::

**5.** A phone is marked NLe 3,000 and sold at a 12% discount. Find the selling price.

:::reveal{title="Show the answer"}
$3\,000 \times 0.88 = $ NLe 2,640.
:::
