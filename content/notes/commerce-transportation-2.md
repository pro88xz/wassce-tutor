# Transport selection, freight costs and logistics performance

## Learning objectives
Compare transport modes, identify factors affecting mode selection and explain how logistics performance influences business cost.

## Comparing modes
| Mode | Strengths | Limitations |
|---|---|---|
| Road | Flexible, door-to-door, suitable for short and medium distances | Congestion, road conditions, fuel cost and limited capacity |
| Rail | Efficient for heavy bulk over suitable routes | Fixed routes, terminals and infrastructure requirements |
| Water | High capacity and relatively low unit cost for bulky international cargo | Slower, port-dependent and exposed to weather and port delays |
| Air | Fast and suitable for urgent or high-value goods | High cost and size/weight restrictions |
| Pipeline | Continuous movement of suitable liquids and gases | High initial cost and limited product suitability |

## Choosing a mode
The decision depends on distance, urgency, weight, volume, value, perishability, security, reliability, infrastructure and total cost. A business exporting cocoa may use road transport to move produce to a port and sea freight for the international journey. A medical consignment with a short shelf life may justify air freight.

## Freight cost
Freight charges may depend on weight, volume, distance, route, fuel, handling, insurance and seasonal demand. For some cargo, the charge is based on whichever is greater: actual weight or volumetric weight. Businesses should compare the full door-to-door cost, not only the headline freight rate.

## Logistics performance
Useful measures include:
- on-time delivery rate;
- damage or loss rate;
- average transit time;
- cost per unit delivered;
- vehicle or container utilisation;
- order fulfilment accuracy.

A low transport rate may be poor value if delays cause production stoppages or stock-outs. Reliable scheduling and proper packaging can reduce total supply-chain cost.

## WASSCE examination focus
When asked to recommend a mode, identify the nature of the goods and explain why the chosen mode suits them. Avoid saying one mode is always the cheapest or fastest in every circumstance.

## Quick check
1. State four factors affecting transport choice.
2. Which mode is generally suitable for urgent high-value cargo?
3. Give two limitations of water transport.
4. Why should a firm measure on-time delivery?

**Answers:** 1. Distance, urgency, cost, weight, volume, value, perishability and infrastructure. 2. Air transport. 3. Slow speed, port dependence and weather or port delays. 4. It shows reliability and helps identify delays that can cause stock-outs or customer dissatisfaction.
