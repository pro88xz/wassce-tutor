The main **environmental problems** in West Africa are **deforestation**, **desertification**, **soil erosion**, **pollution** of air, water and land, **flooding**, **coastal erosion**, **bush burning** and the **loss of wildlife**. Most are caused or worsened by human activities such as farming, logging, charcoal burning, mining, overgrazing and the rapid growth of towns. This lesson explains the causes, effects and solutions of each problem, with Sierra Leone and Sahel examples.

## Learning objectives

By the end of this lesson you should be able to:

- define environment and environmental degradation
- explain the causes, effects and control of deforestation
- explain the causes, effects and control of desertification
- describe the types, causes and effects of pollution
- describe other environmental problems: flooding, coastal erosion, bush burning, loss of biodiversity
- describe environmental problems in Sierra Leone

## The environment

:::callout{type=definition}
The **environment** is everything around us: land, water, air, plants, animals and the things people have built. **Environmental degradation** is the damage to or loss of the quality of the environment, mostly through human activity.
:::

## Deforestation

**Deforestation** is the clearing of forests faster than they can regrow.

| Causes | Effects |
|---|---|
| clearing for farms (shifting cultivation, bush fallowing, plantations) | **soil erosion** and loss of fertility |
| **logging** for timber | **loss of wildlife** and plant species |
| **firewood and charcoal** for cooking | **landslides and floods** on bare slopes |
| **mining** (open pits, alluvial digging) | less rainfall locally and drying of streams |
| **urban expansion**, roads and dams | more **carbon dioxide** in the air (climate change) |
| **bush fires** | loss of medicines, food and income from the forest |

**Control:** afforestation and reforestation; agroforestry; forest reserves and national parks; laws against illegal logging; alternative cooking fuels (LPG, improved stoves); education.

:::callout{type=local-example}
Sierra Leone has lost much of its original forest. The **Western Area Peninsula forest**, which protects Freetown's water supply, is under heavy pressure from **house building and charcoal burning**. The deadly **Regent mudslide of August 2017** in Freetown showed how building on cleared, steep slopes increases the risk of landslides. Remaining large forests are protected in reserves such as **Gola Rainforest National Park**.
:::

## Desertification

*Figure 1. Causes and effects of desertification. WASSCE Tutor diagram.*

**Desertification** is the spread of desert-like conditions into dry lands, especially the **Sahel**, the semi-arid belt south of the Sahara (northern Senegal, Mali, Burkina Faso, Niger, northern Nigeria, Chad).

- **Causes:** **overgrazing**, **over-cultivation**, **cutting trees for fuel**, **bush burning**, **drought** and **climate change**, poor irrigation that causes salty soils, and **population growth**.
- **Effects:** loss of farmland and pasture, **food shortage and famine**, **migration**, **dust storms**, drying of rivers and lakes (Lake Chad has shrunk greatly since the 1960s), and **conflicts** over land and water.
- **Control:** planting **shelter belts** and trees; the **Great Green Wall** across the Sahel, launched by the African Union in **2007**; **controlled grazing** and fewer animals; **drought-resistant crops**; soil and water conservation (stone lines, zai pits); alternative fuels; irrigation.

## Pollution

| Type | Causes | Effects |
|---|---|---|
| **Air** | vehicle exhaust, generators, factory smoke, burning rubbish, bush fires, cooking smoke | breathing diseases, acid rain, global warming |
| **Water** | sewage, rubbish, oil spills, mining waste, chemicals, fertilisers | waterborne diseases (cholera, typhoid), death of fish |
| **Land (soil)** | plastic and solid waste, mining spoil, chemicals | loss of farmland, breeding of disease carriers, blocked drains |
| **Noise** | traffic, generators, loudspeakers, factories | stress, hearing loss |

The **Niger Delta** in Nigeria suffers severe oil pollution from spills, pipeline leaks and gas flaring.

:::callout{type=local-example}
In **Freetown**, solid waste goes mainly to dumpsites at **Kingtom** and **Granville Brook**, where burning rubbish pollutes the air; **plastic waste** blocks drains and worsens flooding; and **sewage** and rubbish pollute streams and the sea. In mining areas, river silt and abandoned pits harm water and farmland.
:::

## Other environmental problems

1. **Flooding:** heavy rain, blocked drains, building in waterways and deforestation cause floods in cities such as Freetown, Accra and Lagos.
2. **Coastal erosion:** waves, sand mining from beaches, mangrove cutting and rising sea levels wear away coasts and threaten settlements.
3. **Bush burning:** for hunting, land clearing and pasture; destroys vegetation, kills wildlife, exposes soil and pollutes the air.
4. **Loss of biodiversity:** hunting, habitat destruction and pollution threaten species such as chimpanzees, elephants and pygmy hippos.
5. **Overfishing:** too many fish caught, including by illegal foreign trawlers, reducing fish stocks.
6. **Soil erosion** (see the soils lesson).

:::callout{type=common-mistake}
Desertification is not the same as a **desert moving forward** on its own. It is mainly the **degradation of dry land** by human activities such as overgrazing and tree cutting, made worse by drought.
:::

## Key points

- **Deforestation:** farming, logging, charcoal, mining, towns; causes erosion, loss of wildlife, landslides, climate change.
- **Desertification** in the **Sahel**: overgrazing, over-cultivation, fuelwood, burning, drought; controlled by tree planting (**Great Green Wall, 2007**), grazing control and conservation.
- **Pollution:** air, water, land and noise; **Kingtom** and **Granville Brook** dumpsites; **Niger Delta** oil spills.
- Others: **flooding, coastal erosion, bush burning, biodiversity loss, overfishing**.

## WASSCE examination focus

:::callout{type=waec-tests}
Expect to **explain the causes and effects of a named environmental problem** (deforestation, desertification, pollution, flooding) and to **suggest measures to control it**. Using Sierra Leone examples (the Western Area forest, the 2017 mudslide, Freetown's dumpsites) and the Sahel for desertification earns high marks.
:::

## Quick check

1. Define deforestation.
2. Give three causes of deforestation in Sierra Leone.
3. What is the Sahel?
4. State two measures to control desertification.
5. Give two causes of water pollution.

**Answers:**

1. The clearing of forests faster than they can regrow.
2. Any three: farming; logging; firewood and charcoal; mining; urban expansion; bush fires.
3. The semi-arid belt of land along the southern edge of the Sahara Desert.
4. Any two: planting trees and shelter belts (Great Green Wall); controlled grazing; drought-resistant crops; soil and water conservation; alternative fuels; irrigation.
5. Any two: sewage; rubbish; oil spills; mining waste; industrial chemicals; fertilisers.
