The main **modes of transport** are **road**, **rail**, **water** (sea, river and lake), **air** and **pipeline**. Each has its own **advantages and disadvantages** in cost, speed, capacity, flexibility and safety: water transport is the cheapest for bulky goods, air the fastest, road the most flexible, rail the best for heavy loads over land, and pipelines the cheapest way to move liquids and gas. This lesson compares the modes, explains the factors that affect transport development, and outlines the importance of transport.

## Learning objectives

By the end of this lesson you should be able to:

- define transport and communication
- describe road, rail, water, air and pipeline transport
- state the advantages and disadvantages of each mode
- explain the factors that influence the development of transport
- explain the importance of transport

## Comparing the modes

![Transport modes compared on a graph of speed against cost per tonne: water slowest and cheapest, then pipeline, rail, road, and air fastest and most expensive](https://wasscetutor.com/img/geography/transport-cost-speed.svg)

*Figure 1. Comparing transport modes by speed and cost. WASSCE Tutor diagram.*

### Road transport

| Advantages | Disadvantages |
|---|---|
| **door-to-door** service, flexible routes and times | accidents are common |
| cheap to build compared with railways | not suited to very heavy, bulky goods over long distances |
| fast over short and medium distances | congestion in towns |
| reaches remote villages | roads damaged by heavy rain and overloading |
| good for perishable goods | pollution and high fuel costs |

### Rail transport

| Advantages | Disadvantages |
|---|---|
| carries **heavy, bulky goods** cheaply over long distances (iron ore, bauxite) | very expensive to build and maintain |
| carries many passengers at once | fixed routes; no door-to-door service |
| fewer accidents than road | goods must be moved again by road at each end |
| not affected by road congestion | difficult to build in mountains and swamps |

### Water transport

| Advantages | Disadvantages |
|---|---|
| **cheapest** for bulky goods over long distances | **slowest** mode |
| ocean ships carry most of world trade | ports are expensive to build |
| natural routes (seas, rivers, lakes) need little building | rivers may have rapids, waterfalls, sandbars or low water in the dry season |
| | risk of storms and piracy |

### Air transport

| Advantages | Disadvantages |
|---|---|
| **fastest** mode, especially over long distances | **most expensive** per tonne and per passenger |
| crosses mountains, forests, deserts and seas | carries limited, light loads |
| good for perishable, valuable and urgent goods (flowers, medicines, mail) | needs airports, often far from town centres |
| | affected by bad weather; high fuel cost |

### Pipeline transport

| Advantages | Disadvantages |
|---|---|
| cheap to run once built | expensive to build |
| continuous flow of liquids and gases | carries only liquids, gases and slurries |
| not affected by weather or traffic | leaks, theft and sabotage (oil bunkering in the Niger Delta) |

The **West African Gas Pipeline** carries natural gas from Nigeria through Benin and Togo to Ghana.

## Factors influencing transport development

| Factor | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Relief** | mountains, steep slopes and escarpments make roads and railways costly; plains are easy |
| **Climate** | heavy rain washes away roads; seasonal floods and low water affect rivers |
| **Vegetation** | dense forest is costly to clear |
| **Drainage** | swamps and wide rivers need bridges and causeways |
| **Economic activities** | mines, plantations and industries need transport (iron-ore railways to the ports) |
| **Population** | densely populated areas attract roads |
| **Capital and technology** | building and maintaining routes is expensive |
| **Government policy** | national plans, regional integration |

## Importance of transport

1. Moves **goods** from producers to markets.
2. Moves **people** to work, school, markets and services.
3. Enables **trade**, at home and abroad.
4. Opens up **remote areas** for development.
5. Supports **industry, mining and agriculture**.
6. Creates **jobs** (drivers, port workers, mechanics).
7. Promotes **national unity** and **regional integration**.
8. Supports **tourism**.

:::callout{type=local-example}
In **Sierra Leone**, **road** transport carries most passengers and goods. Iron ore from Tonkolili moves by **rail** to **Pepel** for export by **sea**. Most imports arrive by sea at the **Queen Elizabeth II Quay** in Freetown. **Lungi International Airport**, across the Sierra Leone River estuary from Freetown, handles international flights, and ferries and water taxis link it to the city. Motorbike taxis (**okadas**) and keke tricycles carry many people in towns and rural areas.
:::

:::callout{type=common-mistake}
Do not say rail is "always cheaper" than road. Rail is cheaper for **heavy, bulky goods over long distances**; for short journeys and small loads, road is usually cheaper and more convenient.
:::

## Key points

- Modes: **road, rail, water, air, pipeline**.
- **Water:** cheapest, slowest. **Air:** fastest, dearest. **Road:** flexible, door-to-door. **Rail:** heavy loads over long distances. **Pipeline:** liquids and gases.
- Factors: **relief, climate, vegetation, drainage, economic activities, population, capital, government**.
- Transport enables **trade, development, jobs, unity and tourism**.

## WASSCE examination focus

:::callout{type=waec-tests}
Expect to **compare modes of transport** (advantages and disadvantages), to **explain the factors affecting the development of transport** in West Africa, and to **state the importance of transport**. ## Quick check

1. Which mode of transport is cheapest for bulky goods over long distances?
2. Give two advantages of road transport.
3. Why is air transport used for medicines and fresh flowers?
4. Name a pipeline in West Africa.
5. How does relief affect transport development?

**Answers:**

1. Water transport.
2. Any two: door-to-door service; flexible; cheaper to build than rail; fast over short distances; reaches remote areas.
3. It is the fastest mode, so perishable and urgent goods arrive quickly.
4. The West African Gas Pipeline (Nigeria to Ghana).
5. Mountains and steep slopes make roads and railways difficult and expensive to build; plains make building easy and cheap.
