Twenty years ago a trader in Freetown queued in a bank to pay a supplier, ordered goods by letter and knew customers only by face. Today the same trader pays by phone, advertises on social media, sells to customers in other towns and may run a franchise of an international brand. This lesson surveys the **modern trends** that are reshaping commerce, and the security risks that come with them.

## Learning objectives

By the end of this lesson you should be able to:

- describe modern trends in banking and payments
- explain the use of ICT in business
- explain franchising, self-service and other modern trends in retailing
- explain globalisation and regional integration
- state the effects of modern trends on commerce
- explain how businesses and consumers stay safe online

## Modern trends in banking and payments

| Trend | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Mobile money** | Sending, receiving, saving and paying through mobile phone wallets |
| **Mobile and internet banking** | Customers manage accounts, transfer money and pay bills by app or computer |
| **ATMs** | Machines for cash withdrawals, balance checks and transfers at any time |
| **POS terminals** | Card payment machines in shops, hotels and filling stations |
| **Debit and credit cards** | Cashless payment, in person and online |
| **Agency banking** | Shops act as agents for banks, taking deposits and paying withdrawals |
| **SMS and app alerts** | Instant notice of every transaction |
| **Cashless policies** | Governments encourage electronic payments to reduce cash handling |

## ICT in business

**Information and communication technology (ICT)** is used to:

1. **Keep records**: accounting software, stock control, payroll.
2. **Communicate**: email, video calls, WhatsApp groups with staff and suppliers.
3. **Market** products: websites, social media, digital advertising.
4. **Sell** goods online (e-commerce).
5. **Manage stock**: **barcodes** and scanners at supermarket tills update stock automatically.
6. **Make payments** electronically.
7. **Carry out research** on markets and competitors.
8. **Work remotely** and store data in the cloud.

**Digital marketing** uses social media pages, influencers, targeted adverts, SMS campaigns and search engines. It is cheap, measurable and reaches particular groups.

## Modern trends in retailing and distribution

| Trend | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Supermarkets and shopping malls** | Large self-service stores and centres with many shops under one roof |
| **Self-service** | Customers pick goods themselves and pay at a checkout; fewer staff, lower costs |
| **Franchising** | See below |
| **E-commerce and home delivery** | Online orders delivered to the door |
| **Containerisation** | Standard sealed containers moved door to door |
| **Just-in-time stock** | Supplies arrive exactly when needed, reducing storage costs |
| **Branding and pre-packing** | Goods packed and branded by the manufacturer |
| **Outsourcing** | Firms hire outside firms for tasks such as cleaning, security, delivery and IT |
| **Hire purchase and credit facilities** | Buying now and paying later, including "buy now, pay later" schemes online |

### Franchising

:::callout{type=definition}
**Franchising** is an arrangement in which a firm (the **franchisor**) allows another business (the **franchisee**) to use its **name, brand, products and methods** in return for a **fee** and **royalties** (a share of sales).
:::

| Advantages to the franchisee | Disadvantages to the franchisee |
|---|---|
| A **known brand** attracts customers from the start | Must pay **fees and royalties** |
| **Training** and support from the franchisor | **Little freedom**: must follow the franchisor's rules |
| Lower risk of failure | The franchisor's mistakes harm the franchisee's reputation |
| Shared national advertising | Profits are shared |

Fast-food chains, filling stations and mobile phone shops often operate as franchises or agencies of larger brands.

## Globalisation and regional integration

:::callout{type=definition}
**Globalisation** is the growing **interconnection of the world's economies** through trade, investment, technology and communication.
:::

- **Effects:** wider markets, foreign investment, cheaper imports and new technology. But local firms face strong foreign competition, and economies become exposed to world price shocks.
- **Regional integration:** **ECOWAS** promotes free movement of goods, people and services in West Africa. The **African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA)**, of which Sierra Leone is a member, aims to create a single African market by reducing tariffs between member countries.

## Effects of modern trends on commerce

| Positive effects | Negative effects |
|---|---|
| **Faster, cheaper transactions** | **Job losses** as machines and self-service replace workers |
| **Wider markets** for small firms | **Elimination of middlemen**, such as some wholesalers |
| **Less cash handling** and theft | **Cybercrime and fraud** |
| **Better records** and decisions | Need for **new skills**; those without them are left behind |
| More **choice** and **convenience** for consumers | **Dependence on power and networks**; systems fail during outages |
| Easier access to **credit** and banking for the poor | **Privacy** risks from data collection |

## Staying safe online

**Common threats:**

- **Phishing**: fake messages or websites that trick people into giving PINs, passwords or one-time codes.
- **Fake mobile money messages**: "You have received NLe 500, please send it back."
- **SIM swap fraud**: criminals take over a victim's phone number to reach their accounts.
- **Fake online sellers** who take payment and never deliver.
- **Hacking and malware**, which steal data or lock computers.

**Good practice:**

1. **Never share** your PIN, password or **one-time code (OTP)**, not even with someone claiming to be from the bank or network.
2. **Check your balance** before acting on any "money received" message.
3. Use **strong passwords** and **two-step verification**.
4. Buy from **trusted sellers**; check reviews; use cash on delivery for new sellers.
5. Keep phones and computers **updated**, and install software only from trusted sources.
6. **Report** fraud to the bank, the mobile network and the police at once.

:::callout{type=worked-example}
**Spot the fraud.** A trader receives an SMS: "You have received NLe 2,000 from 07X XXX XXX. Ref 88231." Minutes later a caller says he sent it by mistake and begs her to send it back.

**What should she do?** Check her **actual mobile money balance** in the official app or menu, not the SMS. Real transaction messages come from the network's official sender name, not an ordinary phone number. If no money arrived, it is a **fraud attempt**: send nothing and report the number to the network.
:::

:::callout{type=local-example}
In Sierra Leone, mobile money, POS terminals in Freetown supermarkets and banking apps are fast replacing cash for many payments. Government services are moving online too. Mobile money fraud has risen alongside these changes, and networks regularly warn customers **never to share their PIN** and to ignore "wrong transfer" calls.
:::

:::callout{type=common-mistake}
"Modern trends" answers should be **explained**, not just listed. Say what the trend is **and** how it affects business, for example: "POS terminals let shops accept card payments, so they hold less cash and reduce theft."
:::

## Key points

- Banking and payment trends: **mobile money, mobile and internet banking, ATMs, POS, cards, agency banking**.
- **ICT** is used for records, communication, marketing, selling, stock control and payments.
- Retail trends: **supermarkets, self-service, franchising, e-commerce, just-in-time, outsourcing**.
- **Globalisation**, **ECOWAS** and **AfCFTA** widen markets but increase competition.
- Modern trends bring **speed, reach and convenience**, but also **job losses, cybercrime and new skill needs**; never share PINs or OTPs.

## WASSCE examination focus

Expect to **explain modern trends in commerce** (e-banking, ATMs, POS, mobile money, franchising, containerisation, globalisation), to **state their advantages and disadvantages**, and to **explain the effects of ICT on business**. Explain each trend in a full sentence and give its effect.
:::
