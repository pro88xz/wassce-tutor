# How aids to trade work together in a modern supply chain

## Learning objectives
Analyse the interdependence of aids to trade and explain how disruptions affect the movement of goods and payments.

## A connected system
Aids to trade are services that make buying, selling and distribution possible. Their value is greatest when they work together.

Consider a Sierra Leonean importer bringing packaged food into the country:
1. **Communication** carries quotations, orders, shipping updates and complaints.
2. **Banking and payment systems** transfer funds and provide trade finance.
3. **Transport** moves goods from the port to warehouses and shops.
4. **Warehousing** protects stock and balances supply with demand.
5. **Insurance** covers specified losses such as fire, theft or marine risks, subject to policy terms.
6. **Advertising and market information** help customers identify products and help sellers forecast demand.

## Interdependence and risk
A failure in one service can affect the whole chain. A delayed vessel may increase storage charges. Poor communication may leave a retailer unaware of the delay and cause stock-outs. Inadequate storage may damage goods even when transport is reliable. A payment delay may prevent an importer from releasing cargo.

Businesses manage these risks through supplier checks, accurate records, suitable insurance, alternative transport arrangements, stock monitoring and clear communication.

## Cost and customer value
The final selling price includes more than the factory price. It may include freight, handling, customs-related charges, storage, finance, insurance, marketing, distribution and retailer costs. A business should compare the total landed and distribution cost rather than selecting a supplier only because its quoted price is low.

**Landed cost** is the total cost of bringing goods to the specified destination, including relevant purchase, freight, insurance, handling and import charges.

## WASSCE examination focus
Questions may ask you to explain the importance of several aids to trade. Avoid giving isolated definitions only: show how one service supports another and how the chain serves producers and consumers.

1. Name six aids to trade.
2. Explain how banking supports foreign trade.
3. What is meant by landed cost?
4. Give one example of how communication failure can disrupt distribution.

**Answers:** 1. Transport, communication, warehousing, banking, insurance and advertising. 2. It enables payments, foreign exchange services and finance. 3. The total relevant cost of bringing goods to a destination. 4. A retailer may not receive delay information and may fail to arrange replacement stock.
