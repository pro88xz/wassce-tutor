# Warehouse management, stock rotation and inventory costs

## Learning objectives
Compare warehouse types, explain stock rotation and analyse the costs of holding inventory.

## Types of warehouse
A **private warehouse** is owned or controlled by a business for its own goods. A **public warehouse** stores goods for customers in return for a fee. A **bonded warehouse** stores imported goods under customs control before applicable duties or conditions are settled. A **cold-storage warehouse** maintains low temperatures for products requiring refrigeration.

## Warehouse functions
Warehouses protect goods, consolidate consignments, break bulk, facilitate sorting and grading, support packaging and provide a buffer between production and demand. A well-managed warehouse also maintains accurate inventory records and supports traceability.

## Inventory costs
Holding stock has several costs:
- capital tied up in goods;
- rent, utilities and warehouse labour;
- insurance and security;
- damage, theft and deterioration;
- obsolescence and expiry;
- ordering and handling costs.

The business balances the cost of holding stock against the cost of ordering too frequently or running out.

## Stock rotation
**FIFO** means first in, first out; older stock is issued first. **FEFO** means first expiry, first out; stock with the earliest expiry date is issued first. FEFO is especially important for food, medicines and other dated products.

A stocktaking exercise physically counts inventory and compares it with records. Differences may arise from theft, damage, recording errors or unrecorded transactions and should be investigated.

## Warehouse layout and safety
Goods should be labelled and placed so that staff can locate them quickly. Heavy items need safe storage; walkways and emergency exits must remain clear. Fire precautions, pest control, ventilation and temperature monitoring should suit the goods stored.

## WASSCE examination focus
Match the warehouse type to the goods or purpose. Explain both the benefit and cost of storage; warehousing is useful but is not free.
