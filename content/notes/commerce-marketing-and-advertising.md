# Marketing and advertising: customer needs and the marketing mix

## Learning objectives
Distinguish marketing from advertising, explain the marketing mix and identify common methods of research and promotion.

## Marketing and advertising
Marketing is the process of understanding customer needs and creating, communicating, delivering and exchanging value. It includes market research, product decisions, pricing, distribution, promotion and customer relationships. Advertising is only one part of promotion, and promotion is only one part of marketing.

A business should identify the customers it intends to serve. Market segmentation divides a broad market into groups with similar characteristics, such as location, income, needs or buying behaviour. Targeting selects the group or groups the business will serve. Positioning describes the value and identity the business wants customers to associate with its offer.

## The marketing mix: the 4 Ps

| Element | Main decision | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Product | What need will the good or service satisfy? | Quality, design, packaging and support |
| Price | What will customers pay? | Costs, competitor prices, value and discounts |
| Place | Where and how will it be available? | Shops, distributors, delivery and online channels |
| Promotion | How will customers learn about it? | Advertising, sales promotion and personal selling |

For services, an extended 7 Ps framework adds people, process and physical evidence. These help a business consider staff behaviour, service delivery and visible evidence of quality.

## Market research
Primary research collects new information through surveys, interviews, observation or focus groups. Secondary research uses existing information such as official statistics, industry reports and published studies. Research should use suitable samples and fair questions; a small biased survey cannot reliably represent an entire market.

## Advertising and promotion
Advertising is paid, non-personal communication from an identified sponsor through radio, television, newspapers, outdoor displays or digital platforms. Sales promotion uses short-term incentives such as discounts or coupons. Personal selling involves direct interaction with customers. Public relations builds relationships and reputation. Promotion should be truthful and appropriate to the target audience.

A local food processor may research demand, improve packaging, set a sustainable price, distribute through shops and markets, and use radio or digital advertising. A campaign that attracts buyers but leaves the firm unable to cover costs is not commercially sustainable.

## WASSCE examination focus
Do not define marketing as advertising. When explaining the 4 Ps, describe the business decision represented by each P and apply it to a product or service.
