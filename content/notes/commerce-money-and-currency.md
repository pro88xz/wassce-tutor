# Money and currency: functions, forms and monetary systems

## Learning objectives
Define money, state its functions, identify qualities of good money and explain how money overcomes barter's limitations.

## Meaning of money
Money is anything generally accepted as a means of payment for goods and services and for settling debts within a community or monetary system. Currency usually refers to notes and coins issued or authorised as legal tender by a country's monetary authority. Not every asset is money: an item must be sufficiently accepted and usable for payment to perform money's central functions.

## Functions of money
1. **Medium of exchange:** people use money to buy and sell without finding someone who wants exactly what they offer.
2. **Unit of account:** prices can be expressed in a common unit, making comparison and accounting easier.
3. **Store of value:** purchasing power can be carried into the future, although inflation may reduce it.
4. **Standard of deferred payment:** debts and future payments can be stated in monetary terms.

Money solves the double coincidence of wants problem in barter. Under barter, a farmer wanting shoes would need to find a shoemaker who wants the farmer's produce at the same time. Money allows the farmer to sell produce to one person and buy shoes from another.

## Qualities of good money
Good money should be generally acceptable, durable, portable, divisible, recognisable and relatively stable in value. It should be difficult to counterfeit and convenient to use. No form of money is perfect in every situation.

## Forms of money
Commodity money has value partly because of the material from which it is made. Metallic money uses coins. Paper currency consists of notes. Bank deposits can be transferred electronically and are widely used for payments. Electronic and mobile-money systems allow users to store or transfer monetary value through providers, subject to relevant rules and safeguards.

In Sierra Leone, the leone is the national currency. Mobile-money services can make payments and transfers more accessible, but a mobile-money balance is not the same thing as physical banknotes. Users should verify the provider, recipient and transaction confirmation.

## Purchasing power
Inflation is a sustained increase in the general price level. When prices rise, a fixed amount of money generally buys fewer goods and services. This reduces money's effectiveness as a store of value. Purchasing power is affected by price changes, confidence, monetary conditions and the wider economy.

## WASSCE examination focus
Learn the four main functions and explain each with an example. Do not confuse money with wealth: money is one form of financial asset, while wealth includes a wider range of valuable assets.
