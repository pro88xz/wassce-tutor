Two partners share a profit. Six workers build a wall in 10 days. A bus covers 240 km in 4 hours. A traveller changes dollars into leones. All four are ratio problems: **sharing in a ratio**, **inverse proportion**, **rates** and **exchange rates**. This lesson covers the methods WASSCE expects for each.

## Learning objectives

By the end of this lesson you should be able to:

- share a quantity in a given ratio, including three-part ratios
- find the total or another share when one share or a difference is known
- combine two ratios into one
- solve direct and inverse proportion problems
- work with rates, including speed, unit conversions and exchange rates

## Sharing in a ratio

**Method:** add the parts, find the value of one part, then multiply.

![Two boxes for Fatmata and three for Musa, each worth NLe 120, sharing NLe 600 in the ratio 2 : 3](https://wasscetutor.com/img/mathematics/ratio-sharing-bars.svg)

*Figure 1. Sharing NLe 600 in the ratio 2 : 3 with a bar model. WASSCE Tutor diagram.*

:::callout{type=worked-example}
**Share NLe 600 between Fatmata and Musa in the ratio 2 : 3.**

Total parts $= 5$; one part $= 600 \div 5 = 120$.

Fatmata: $2 \times 120 = $ NLe 240. Musa: $3 \times 120 = $ NLe 360. (Check: $240 + 360 = 600$ ✓.)
:::

:::callout{type=worked-example}
**Ade and Bintu share money in the ratio 3 : 5. Bintu gets NLe 400 more than Ade. Find the total.**

The difference is $5 - 3 = 2$ parts $= 400$, so one part $= 200$.

Total $= 8 \times 200 = $ NLe 1,600 (Ade NLe 600, Bintu NLe 1,000).
:::

:::callout{type=worked-example}
**Share NLe 1,800 in the ratio 2 : 3 : 4.**

Parts $= 9$; one part $= 200$. Shares: NLe 400, NLe 600 and NLe 800.
:::

### Combining ratios

If $a : b = 2 : 3$ and $b : c = 4 : 5$, make the common term $b$ the same in both: multiply the first by 4 and the second by 3.

$$a : b = 8 : 12, \quad b : c = 12 : 15 \quad\Rightarrow\quad a : b : c = 8 : 12 : 15$$

## Direct proportion

Two quantities are in **direct proportion** if they increase at the same rate. Use the **unitary method**: find the value for one unit first.

:::callout{type=worked-example}
**5 bags of rice cost NLe 1,750. Find the cost of 8 bags.**

One bag costs $1\,750 \div 5 = 350$, so 8 bags cost $8 \times 350 = $ NLe 2,800.
:::

## Inverse proportion

Two quantities are in **inverse proportion** if one increases as the other decreases so that their **product stays constant**.

:::callout{type=worked-example}
**6 workers build a wall in 10 days. How long would 4 workers take, working at the same rate?**

Total work $= 6 \times 10 = 60$ worker-days. With 4 workers: $60 \div 4 = 15$ days.
:::

:::callout{type=common-mistake}
Fewer workers need **more** days, not fewer. If your answer for 4 workers is less than 10 days, you have treated an inverse proportion as direct.
:::

## Rates

A **rate** compares two quantities measured in different units, such as km per hour or litres per minute.

### Speed

$$\text{speed} = \frac{\text{distance}}{\text{time}} \qquad \text{average speed} = \frac{\text{total distance}}{\text{total time}}$$

:::callout{type=worked-example}
**(a) A bus travels 240 km in 4 hours. Find its average speed in km/h and in m/s.**

$240 \div 4 = 60$ km/h. Since 1 km/h $= \dfrac{1000}{3600}$ m/s, $60$ km/h $= \dfrac{60\,000}{3600} = 16.7$ m/s (1 d.p.).

**(b) A driver goes 120 km at 60 km/h, then 120 km at 40 km/h. Find the average speed for the whole journey.**

Times: $2$ h and $3$ h. Average speed $= \dfrac{240}{5} = 48$ km/h (not 50, the average of the two speeds).
:::

### Other rates

:::callout{type=worked-example}
**A tank holding 1,200 litres is filled at 15 litres per minute. How long does it take?**

$1\,200 \div 15 = 80$ minutes, or 1 hour 20 minutes.
:::

### Exchange rates

:::callout{type=local-example}
**Suppose a Freetown bureau's rate is 1 US dollar = NLe 23 (an example rate; real rates change daily).**

- 150 dollars buys $150 \times 23 = $ NLe 3,450.
- NLe 920 buys $920 \div 23 = 40$ dollars.

To convert **into leones**, multiply by the rate; to convert **out of leones**, divide.
:::

## Key points

- **Sharing:** add the parts, find one part, multiply.
- A **difference** between shares gives the value of the difference in parts.
- **Combine ratios** by making the shared term equal.
- **Direct proportion:** unitary method. **Inverse proportion:** the product is constant.
- **Average speed** = total distance ÷ total time; $1$ km/h $= \tfrac{5}{18}$ m/s.

## WASSCE examination focus

:::callout{type=waec-tests}
Expect **sharing problems** (including given one share or a difference), **three-part ratios**, **workers-and-days inverse proportion**, **average speed over two stages**, and **currency conversion**. Write down the value of one part or one unit clearly before finishing.
:::

## Practice

Try each question first, then open the answer to check your working.

**1.** Share NLe 2,400 in the ratio 5 : 7.

:::reveal{title="Show the answer"}
One part $= 200$: NLe 1,000 and NLe 1,400.
:::

**2.** In the ratio 4 : 9, the larger share is NLe 450. Find the smaller share.

:::reveal{title="Show the answer"}
One part $= 50$, so the smaller share is NLe 200.
:::

**3.** 8 men dig a trench in 6 days. How long would 12 men take?

:::reveal{title="Show the answer"}
$48 \div 12 = 4$ days.
:::

**4.** Convert 72 km/h to m/s.

:::reveal{title="Show the answer"}
$72 \times \tfrac{5}{18} = 20$ m/s.
:::

**5.** If $p : q = 3 : 4$ and $q : r = 6 : 5$, find $p : q : r$.

:::reveal{title="Show the answer"}
$9 : 12 : 10$.
:::
