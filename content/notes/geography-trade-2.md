**Tourism** is travel by people to places outside their usual home for leisure, business or culture, and the industry that serves them. **Sierra Leone's tourist attractions** include white-sand **beaches** on the Freetown Peninsula, the **Banana Islands** and **Turtle Islands**, historic **Bunce Island**, wildlife at **Tacugama Chimpanzee Sanctuary**, **Tiwai Island** and **Gola Rainforest National Park**, and the mountains of **Loma** and **Bintumani**. Tourism earns foreign exchange and creates jobs, but it is held back by poor infrastructure, unreliable power and high travel costs. This lesson describes the attractions, importance, problems and effects of tourism.

## Learning objectives

By the end of this lesson you should be able to:

- define tourism and classify types of tourism
- explain the factors that attract tourists
- describe the main tourist attractions of Sierra Leone
- describe tourism elsewhere in West Africa
- explain the importance of tourism
- explain the problems of tourism and its negative effects, and suggest solutions

## Types of tourism

| Type | Example |
|---|---|
| **Beach (coastal)** | sun, sea and sand at River No. 2 or Tokeh |
| **Ecotourism (nature)** | wildlife and forests at Tiwai, Gola and Outamba-Kilimi |
| **Heritage (historical)** | Bunce Island; Cape Coast and Elmina castles in Ghana |
| **Cultural** | festivals, crafts, music, food |
| **Adventure** | mountain climbing on Bintumani; surfing at Bureh Beach |
| **Business and conference** | meetings in Freetown, Lagos, Accra |
| **Diaspora (roots) tourism** | visits by people of African descent tracing their history |

## Factors that attract tourists

1. **Natural attractions:** beaches, mountains, waterfalls, forests, wildlife.
2. **Climate:** warm, sunny weather, especially in the dry season.
3. **History and culture:** castles, museums, festivals, crafts.
4. **Accessibility:** airports, roads and visas.
5. **Accommodation and services:** hotels, restaurants, guides.
6. **Peace and security.**
7. **Cost:** affordable travel and stay.
8. **Marketing** and a positive image.

## Tourist attractions of Sierra Leone

:::callout{type=local-example}
- **Beaches** on the Freetown Peninsula: **Lumley**, **Lakka**, **River No. 2**, **Tokeh**, **Bureh** (known for surfing) and **Sussex**.
- **Islands:** the **Banana Islands** off the peninsula, the **Turtle Islands** off Bonthe, and **Bunce Island** in the Sierra Leone River, a slave-trading fort active in the 17th and 18th centuries, now an important heritage site for the African diaspora.
- **Wildlife and forests:** **Tacugama Chimpanzee Sanctuary** in the Western Area Peninsula forest, **Tiwai Island Wildlife Sanctuary** on the Moa River (rich in primates and home to the pygmy hippopotamus), **Gola Rainforest National Park** on the Liberian border, and **Outamba-Kilimi National Park** in the north.
- **Mountains:** the **Loma Mountains** and **Bintumani**, Sierra Leone's highest point.
- **History and culture:** the **National Museum** in Freetown, Fourah Bay College (founded **1827**, the oldest Western-style university in West Africa), and festivals, crafts and music.
- Freetown's famous **Cotton Tree**, a historic landmark, fell during a storm in **May 2023**.
:::

## Tourism elsewhere in West Africa

- **Ghana:** Cape Coast and Elmina castles, Kakum National Park (canopy walkway), Mole National Park.
- **The Gambia:** beach tourism along the Atlantic coast, popular with European visitors.
- **Senegal:** Gorée Island, Dakar, Saloum Delta.
- **Nigeria:** Yankari Game Reserve, Obudu Mountain Resort, Osun-Osogbo Sacred Grove.
- **Cabo Verde:** beach resorts on Sal and Boa Vista.

## Importance of tourism

1. **Foreign exchange** earnings.
2. **Employment** in hotels, restaurants, transport, guiding and crafts.
3. **Government revenue** from taxes, visas and park fees.
4. **Infrastructure:** roads, airports, power and water that also serve local people.
5. **Market for local goods:** crafts, food, entertainment.
6. **Conservation:** park fees and visitors give reasons to protect forests and wildlife.
7. **Cultural exchange** and international understanding.

## Problems of tourism in Sierra Leone

1. **Poor roads** to many attractions.
2. **Unreliable electricity and water** supply.
3. **Few quality hotels** outside Freetown, and high prices.
4. **Expensive flights** and the difficult journey from Lungi Airport to Freetown.
5. **Negative image** from the civil war and the 2014–2016 Ebola outbreak.
6. **Poor marketing** and limited tourist information.
7. **Shortage of trained staff.**
8. **Waste and pollution** on beaches.

## Negative effects of tourism

1. **Cultural erosion:** local customs and dress changed by foreign influence.
2. **Environmental damage:** litter, beach erosion, building on coasts, disturbance of wildlife.
3. **Social problems:** crime, drug abuse, prostitution.
4. **Leakage:** profits go to foreign-owned hotels and airlines.
5. **Seasonal jobs** and rising prices for local people.

## Developing tourism

1. Improve **roads, power, water** and airport links.
2. Build and upgrade **hotels**, and train hospitality workers.
3. **Market** Sierra Leone abroad and promote diaspora tourism.
4. **Protect** beaches, forests and wildlife through **ecotourism** that benefits local communities.
5. Ensure **security** and easy **visa** processes.

:::callout{type=common-mistake}
Tourism is an **invisible** export: the country earns foreign money for a **service**, not for goods leaving the country. Tourism earnings appear in the **balance of payments**, not the balance of trade.
:::

## Key terms

:::callout{type=definition title="Key terms"}
- **Tourism**: travelling for pleasure, business or culture, and the services provided for visitors.
- **Ecotourism**: nature tourism to see wildlife and forests, such as at Tiwai, Gola and Outamba-Kilimi.
- **Heritage tourism**: visiting historical sites, such as Bunce Island.
- **Diaspora tourism** (roots tourism): visits by people of African descent tracing their history.
:::

## Key points

- Types: **beach, ecotourism, heritage, cultural, adventure, business, diaspora**.
- Sierra Leone: **beaches** (Lumley, River No. 2, Tokeh, Bureh), **Banana and Turtle Islands**, **Bunce Island**, **Tacugama, Tiwai, Gola, Outamba-Kilimi**, **Loma and Bintumani**.
- Importance: **foreign exchange, jobs, revenue, infrastructure, crafts, conservation**.
- Problems: **roads, power, hotels, flights, image, marketing, skills**; effects include **cultural erosion, pollution, leakage**.
