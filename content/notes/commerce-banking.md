# Banking: institutions, services and the role of banks in commerce

## Learning objectives
Explain the meaning of banking, distinguish central banking from commercial banking and describe common bank services.

## Meaning of banking
Banking is the business of accepting deposits, providing payment services and supplying finance and other financial services under a regulated framework. Banks connect people and organisations that save money with those that need funds, while providing systems for transferring and settling payments. Banking supports commerce by making transactions more convenient and helping businesses manage cash flow.

## Main types of banks
**Central bank:** the monetary authority responsible for functions such as issuing currency, conducting monetary policy, overseeing aspects of the financial system and acting as banker to government and banks. In Sierra Leone, the Bank of Sierra Leone is the central bank.

**Commercial bank:** accepts deposits and provides payment, lending and related services to households and businesses. Services may include current accounts, savings accounts, transfers, debit cards, business loans and trade finance.

**Merchant or investment bank:** may provide corporate finance, arrange securities issues and offer investment advice, depending on the institution and legal framework.

**Development bank:** provides or supports longer-term finance for development priorities and sectors that may not receive sufficient funding from ordinary commercial lending.

**Mortgage bank or housing-finance institution:** specialises in housing finance and mortgage-related services, subject to the country's financial structure and regulations.

## Common services
A current account is commonly used for frequent transactions. A savings account is designed to hold savings and may pay interest under its terms. A fixed or term deposit holds funds for an agreed period. Banks may provide loans, overdrafts, transfers, standing orders, payment cards, foreign-exchange services and documentary credits.

A loan must be repaid under agreed conditions. Before borrowing, a business should assess interest, fees, security, repayment dates and its ability to generate cash. A bank's approval is not proof that an investment is profitable.

## Banks and commerce
Banks provide traceable payment channels, help businesses receive customer payments, support domestic and international transfers and may finance stock or equipment. Customers should protect account credentials, verify payment instructions and report suspicious activity promptly.

## WASSCE examination focus
Distinguish the central bank from commercial banks. The central bank conducts monetary-policy and currency functions and oversees the financial system; commercial banks provide everyday deposit, payment and lending services to customers.
